Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE complete, production-quality primary desktop web app concept for “说说岛 Sayland”, a Chinese children's voice-driven 3D voxel world building app. Target dimensions 1440 x 1024, landscape. Full application screen only, no device mockup or browser chrome, no multiple variants. Intended audience children around 6–10 with parents; joyful, beautifully crafted and readable, not a business dashboard.
Core task: child speaks “帮我造一间红屋顶的小房子”, a friendly AI companion turns it into editable voxel blocks, then child can say “旁边再种两棵树”. React + Three.js feasible UI. Actual central world must look like a real-time orthographic Three.js voxel scene built entirely from crisp cube blocks, not a painted scenic illustration, photoreal render or smooth clay meshes. The terrain is a small stepped grassy island made of cubic blocks with exposed sandy and earth voxel layers, a blue pond, a cream cube-block cottage with stepped terracotta red block roof, green cubic trees, tiny yellow block duck and stepping stones. Ambient occlusion-like gentle contact shadows, warm daylight, subtle square construction grid. Lovely tactile toy-scale detail, restrained count of objects, all clearly buildable by voxel generators. Render this editable world beautifully and LARGE, occupying 65% of screen, easy to orbit. Pale butter/ivory open background with faint mint near the island, warm charcoal text, persimmon orange main action, leaf green, aqua and lavender secondary accents.
Screen layout: airy header on the base surface, left compact original happy cube mascot + wordmark “说说岛” and small Sayland; center current world name “我的第一座小岛”; right restrained “保存作品” button and small settings gear. Thin left floating toolbar with 4 large friendly line icons and short Chinese labels “旋转”, “方块”, “颜色”, “撤销”. Center is spacious 3D canvas directly on background, NO giant rounded wrapper, no dark editor borders. Small zoom plus/minus and reset-view controls lower left of canvas.
Right a narrow airy conversation area about 280px wide, cream/offwhite surface separated subtly, not a nested stack of cards. Top small original smiling cube companion with label “小岛伙伴”. Two speech messages, child “帮我造一间红屋顶的小房子”, companion “小房子盖好啦！还想加点什么？” and one visible small contextual action “换个颜色”. Plenty of breathing room. No chat avatars repeating on every message, no technical metadata.
Along bottom of canvas, generous anchored white rounded voice composer, orange circular microphone button 64px, big label “点一下，说出你的想象” and smaller “也可以打字告诉我…” text field with a send arrow. Above it only two little suggestion chips: “种一棵大树” and “加一条小河”. Interaction story: microphone is one tap to start, tap to finish; not press and hold. Cute but edited: 2 signature motifs maximum, subtle cube/corner accents, no confetti clutter, no extra stars, no side quests, no statistics, no account upsell, no invented AI badges or feature inventory. Large Chinese UI type, warm rounded Chinese sans feel but highly legible, 48px touch targets, careful alignment and whitespace.
This is a real app screenshot concept, not a marketing poster. All labels and controls are code-native implementation targets; main canvas will be real Three.js, never use the screenshot as app background. Include full bottom composer and entire header without clipping. No additional slogans, labels, invented navigation, pill headers, watermarks or unrelated panels.
